Research

We develop theory for nonequilibrium quantum materials—how electrons and lattice vibrations respond when driven by fields, strain, and light. Our work connects quantum geometry, interactions, and topology to measurable signals in transport and ultrafast optics, with a focus on graphene- and TMD-based heterostructures, altermagnets, and layered van der Waals crystals.

  • Nonlinear light–matter response: quantum geometry, harmonic generation, and optical forces in solids.
  • Quantum transport & correlations: collective modes, topological Fermi liquids, and interaction-driven phenomena.
  • Phonons out of equilibrium: chiral phonons, nonlinear phononics, and coherent lattice control.

CV of Dr Habib Rostami

Personal statement

I am a condensed matter physicist and an assistant professor at the University of Bath, specializing in quantum many-body problems for real-world materials using analytical and numerical techniques, with expertise in quantum transport and light–matter interaction in 2D materials.

Positions

  • Assistant professor (Lecturer), University of Bath, Physics Department (2022–present)
  • Researcher (fixed-term assistant professor), Nordita/Stockholm University, Sweden (2019–2022)
  • Postdoctoral researcher, Scuola Normale Superiore & IIT Genova, Italy (2015–2017); Nordita/Stockholm University (2017–2018)

Grants & awards

  • EPSRC New Investigator Award (UKRI) — tuneable nonlinear phononics in layered quantum materials
  • VR Starting Grant (Swedish Research Council) — nonlinear optics & transport in 2D materials
  • Royal Society International Exchange — anomalous acoustoelectric effects with Prof. Robert Blick, Hamburg
  • Best PhD Thesis Award, Iranian Physical Society

Education

  • MSc, University of Tehran (2007–2010). Thesis: Fabrication of optical magnetic mirrors using bent and mushroom-like carbon nanotubes (experimental & theory)
  • BSc, University of Semnan (2004–2007). Solid state physics
