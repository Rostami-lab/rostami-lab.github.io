Personal statement

I am a theoretical condensed-matter physicist and Lecturer in Physics at the University of Bath. My work develops analytical and numerical frameworks for quantum many-body phenomena in real materials, with emphasis on two-dimensional systems, quantum transport, nonlinear optics, and light–matter interactions.

Track record: 51 publications (46 peer-reviewed); h-index 21; >2000 citations; 32 invited talks.
